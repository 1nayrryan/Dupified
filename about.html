Mission

Replica sneaker culture is massive, but the information is scattered. Good batches get buried in subreddit threads. Prices vary wildly between platforms. New buyers don't know where to start.

Dupeify exists to fix that — one search that surfaces the right listings, the right community discussions, and the right price context, all at once. No affiliate deals. No promoted listings. Just results.

How it works
01
You search. Enter any sneaker name — model, colorway, batch, whatever you know.
02
We aggregate. Results are pulled live from DHgate, AliExpress, Weidian, Taobao, PandaVault, and r/Repsneakers.
03
You compare. Filter by platform, sort by price, and open listings directly — or route through a buying agent with one click.
